    Where every cent comes from

    Each line uses a published statutory rate applied to the basket you set. Nothing is modeled.

    • City combined sales tax rates: Avalara city rate pages, minimum combined 2026 rate for each state's largest city, retrieved September 24, 2026. New York City is 8.875% per the New York State Department of Taxation and Finance, because Avalara's minimum rate leaves out the 0.375% Metropolitan Commuter Transportation District tax.
    • State sales tax rates: Tax Foundation, State and Local Sales Tax Rates, Midyear 2026 (as of July 1, 2026).
    • Gasoline: Tax Foundation, Gas Taxes by State, July 2026. Excludes some local fuel taxes and the price effects of programs like cap-and-trade. Federal rate 18.4¢ per gallon.
    • Cell phone: Tax Foundation, Taxes on Wireless Services, 2025 (July 2025, the latest edition). The state-local rate averages the largest city and the capital. Federal USF 13.36%.
    • Beer: Tax Foundation, Beer Taxes by State, 2026 (January 1, 2026). A six-pack of 12 oz cans is 0.5625 gallons. Federal beer excise is not included.
    • Cigarettes: Tax Foundation, Cigarette Taxes by State, 2026 (July 2026). Federal $1.01 per pack. Local cigarette taxes (Chicago and Cook County $4.18 combined, New York City $1.50, Philadelphia $2.00) from Campaign for Tobacco-Free Kids, December 2025.
    • Vaping: Tax Foundation, Vaping Taxes by State, 2026, tax charged on its sample 4-pack of 1.8 mL cartridges.
    • Starting basket: one month for a family of four, which people can then adjust to their own spending. Groceries use the low end of the $1,013 to $1,668 monthly range for a family of four; household goods $300; clothing $200 with every piece under $100; wireless service $100; food delivery $60. The ranking always compares the visitor's basket across all 50 cities.
    • Food delivery: the city's combined sales tax on the food, or New Hampshire's 8.5% Meals and Rentals Tax. Separate local restaurant or meals taxes and taxes on delivery fees are not yet included.
    • Phone plans: the wireless rate applies to mobile service only. Home internet is exempt from state and local taxes under the federal Internet Tax Freedom Act.
    • Clothing: taxed at the city's combined sales tax rate, except in the seven states that exempt everyday clothing. Minnesota, New Jersey, Pennsylvania and Vermont exempt it fully; Massachusetts ($175), New York ($110, including New York City's local tax) and Rhode Island ($250) exempt items under a per-item cap, and every piece in the basket is under $100, so it falls under all three caps. Sales tax holidays are not counted.
    • Groceries: state grocery rates reflect 2026 law (Illinois and Arkansas ended state grocery taxes January 1, 2026; Mississippi 5%; Missouri 1.225%; Tennessee 4%; Alabama 2%). Cities marked ◇ may add a local grocery tax that is not yet included.

    Excise lines count the excise only, not the general sales tax also charged on beer, cigarettes and vapes, so vice totals are understated rather than overstated.
